### 1.0.20-plus.8

- **A Home screen.** The app opens on a greeting and your sites as cards. Click one to pick up where you left off.
- **Pictures of your sites.** Each card shows a screenshot of the site's home page, taken in the background when you preview it. Use ⋯ → Refresh preview to take a new one.
- **Switch sites from the title bar.** Click the site's name for your recent sites, or press ⇧⌘H to go Home.
- **Saving keeps your formatting.** Only the values you change are rewritten, in the style they already had, so changing something and changing it back leaves nothing to publish.
- Fixed: Open from GitHub showed “Couldn't check” for every repository on accounts with many of them. It now finds your Astro sites.
- The update window now shows what's new in each version.
