### 1.0.20-plus.1, the first Astro Editor+

- **Preview your site from one button.** It starts your site's preview and stops it when you press it again or quit.
- **Sign in with GitHub** and **open a site from GitHub**. It's downloaded and made ready to edit.
- **Publish with one button.** The Changes tab shows what you changed, and you can throw away a change, bring in other people's changes, and pick a version when both of you changed the same entry.
- **Branches and pull requests,** with their checks, and a history graph.
