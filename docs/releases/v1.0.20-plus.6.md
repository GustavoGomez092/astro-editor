### 1.0.20-plus.6

- **Spelling and grammar checking.** Mistakes in your text get a soft underline. Point at one, click it, or press Cmd+. to see suggestions. It works offline. Turn it off, choose your English, or edit your dictionary in Settings → General.
- **Colour themes.** Pick Default, Catppuccin or Nord for light and dark mode in Settings → General, or add your own.
- **Rename a file to match its title.** Change an entry's title and an "Update to …" link appears under it. It's also in the command palette and the right-click menu.
- **A Projects screen** listing every site you've opened, and an Open project button on the welcome screen.
- **Clearer collection settings:** each one says whether you set it or it's automatic, and why.
- Fixes: copies of the same site no longer share settings, a site on an unplugged drive isn't mistaken for a moved one, and settings files are saved safely.
